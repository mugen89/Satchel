# Changelog

What changed in each version of Satchel, newest first. The section of a version is also its "What's new" text:
`release.ps1` copies it into the GitHub release, and Settings → Updates shows it in the app.

Write each version as `## <version> — <date>`, with plain lists under `###` headings.

## 1.3.0 — 2026-11-10

### New

- **Sheets** — a spreadsheet tab: formulas with 100 functions, fill, sort, several sheets per workbook and frozen
  rows. Opens and saves Excel files (.xlsx, .xls) and CSV.
- **Import into the Library** — drop files anywhere on the Library. Markdown and text files, Word documents (.docx,
  .doc) and RTF become Markdown with their pictures; Excel and CSV files become tables.
- **Library folders** — folders and subfolders in the left pane. Drag documents and folders onto a folder, or use
  ⋯ → Move to folder. A private folder hides everything in it.
- **Updates** — Settings → Updates checks for a new version, installs it (after checking the download and backing up
  your data) and shows what changed in each version.
- **Key bindings** — Settings → Key bindings: your own keys for every tab and for showing or hiding private items.
- **Dashboard profiles** — different dashboard setups, each with its own icon as a shortcut at the top. Widgets can
  be resized.
- **Board** — notes with rich text and lists, notes from the Notes tab turned into board notes, and videos, games,
  photos and more from other tabs. Single notes or a whole board can be private.
- **Gamble** — a place for what's coming.

### Improved

- Big documents open much faster in the Library, and coming back to the Library tab is instant.
- The sidebar's sections fold away.
- Customizing the dashboard is smoother.
- "Show private items" moved to Settings → Privacy, and Settings lists every keyboard shortcut.

### Fixed

- The last edits of a document are kept when you switch to another document right away.

## 1.2.0 — 2026-11-09

### New

- Today, Clock, Money, Bookmarks, Photos, Watching, Collections, Music, Feeds, Flashcards and Insights tabs.
- Repeating tasks, live cards and video study on boards, a page view in the Library, a Steam import for Games.
- Every item can be marked private; private items hide until you show them.
- Choose which tabs appear in the sidebar, and in what order.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-11-09

- Projects, Library (Markdown), Reading, Games, Sketch and Videos (YouTube channels and a player with timestamped
  notes).
- The board got rich text, images, link cards, connections and a corkboard with swinging strings.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-09

- First version: notes, tasks, calendar, the encrypted password vault, contacts and the board — all in one folder you
  can carry on a flash drive.
